# Project Manual excerpts

SECTION 00 21 13 - INSTRUCTIONS TO BIDDERS
Bid security: each bid shall be accompanied by a bid bond of 10% of the total bid amount.
The successful bidder shall furnish a performance bond of 100% and a payment bond of 100% of the contract price.

SECTION 00 72 00 - GENERAL CONDITIONS
Contract time: 120 calendar days from Notice to Proceed.
Liquidated damages: $500 per calendar day for each day beyond contract time.

SECTION 26 05 19 - LOW-VOLTAGE ELECTRICAL POWER CONDUCTORS AND CABLES
Provide branch circuit wiring for new lighting and security devices per Sheet E-201.

SECTION 26 51 00 - INTERIOR LIGHTING
Replace 640 fluorescent fixtures with LED luminaires and networked lighting controls per Sheet E-101.

SECTION 28 13 00 - ACCESS CONTROL
Provide card readers at 14 doors, two controllers and head-end integration.

SECTION 28 23 00 - VIDEO SURVEILLANCE
Provide 36 IP cameras, PoE switches and a 30-day recording server.
